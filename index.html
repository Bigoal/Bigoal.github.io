FLAGSHIP · APPLIED ML RESEARCH

Content Review Prioritization

A ranking system that decides which of 200K+ pages a content team should review each month, built on FlyRank's search-performance warehouse (55 client accounts) during my ML internship. I benchmarked logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM against a transparent smoothed-CTR rule, using out-of-time validation and client-level bootstrap intervals. The models' global AUC edge (0.60 vs 0.54) came from account size; per account, the simple rule beat chance in 88–92% of accounts vs 42–52% for LightGBM. A negative control also caught label leakage (CV AUC 0.615 → 0.960).
